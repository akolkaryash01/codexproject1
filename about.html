About CitrusCraft

CitrusCraft was born from a simple belief: luxury should restore nature, not deplete it.

Our designers and material scientists work together to craft premium belts, wallets, pockets, bags, and accessories using orange peel leather—an innovative vegan material made from food industry waste.

Every piece is made for longevity, with timeless silhouettes and conscious construction.
