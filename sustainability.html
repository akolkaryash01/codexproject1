Sustainability at the Core

Orange peel waste is collected from agricultural and juice-processing partners, gently dried, milled into a fine powder, and combined with natural resins and plant binders.

The resulting material is shaped and finished into a durable vegan leather that is elegant, cruelty-free, and biodegradable under controlled conditions.

  • Eco-friendly and lower-impact than conventional leather.
  • Biodegradable and free from animal-derived inputs.
  • Reduces agricultural waste while creating premium accessories.
